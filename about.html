THE STORY BEHIND HER VOICE

Language brings us together. Our voices move us forward.

HER VOICE is a conference celebrating the women shaping language, culture, and communication. It is a space to exchange ideas, share knowledge, and imagine what comes next.

OCTOBER 15–17, 2027 PHOENIX, ARIZONA

WHY HER VOICE EXISTS

Every voice has a story.
Every story adds something.

Language is more than the words we use. It shapes how we understand one another, how knowledge is shared, and whose experiences are heard. Across the world, women are helping shape that understanding through translation, interpreting, education, research, technology, and more. HER VOICE brings these women into one conversation.

OUR MISSION

A place to share ideas, knowledge, and experience.

HER VOICE recognizes the contributions women make across language professions. We bring professionals together to exchange knowledge, discuss the issues shaping their fields, and learn from one another.

01

Recognize women's contributions.

Highlight the expertise, research, and professional achievements of women working across language and communication.

02

Connect across professions and perspectives.

Bring together translators, interpreters, educators, researchers, and other language professionals to share experiences and learn from one another.

03

Shape the future of the field.

Make space for discussions about emerging challenges, changing practices, and the future of language work.

WHO SHOULD BE IN THE ROOM

Different fields.
A shared conversation.

HER VOICE welcomes women working in translation, interpreting, localization, linguistics, language education, research, and communication technology. Whether you are an established professional, an educator, a researcher, or just beginning your career, there is a place for your perspective here.
