OUR VENUE

The Solstice Forum

HER VOICE 2027 will take place at The Solstice Forum, our fictional conference venue in downtown Phoenix. The space is designed for inspiring talks, hands-on workshops, and meaningful conversations.

Attendees can connect with other professionals, exchange ideas, and take part in three days of programming focused on women in language professions.
